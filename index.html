ABOUT

Here at Music Atlas podcasts, our mission is to share with you the freshest and most exciting releases as well as crate digging discoveries, everything is carefully curated and mixed by the Music Atlas DJs. From house to techno, from minimal to drum&bass, we will explore the full spectrum of electronic music, with a focus on production quality, original composing ideas or the cutting-edge sounds that are pushing the boundaries of the genres. 

We make sure to post the tracklists for each episode in the description as we know how important it is to know what you listening to. This way, you can easily follow along and discover new artists and tracks that catch your ear.

If you enjoy our show, we encourage you to follow us on social media to stay up-to-date with our latest releases and events. Sharing our content with your friends and fellow music lovers also helps us spread the word and support the artists we feature on the show.
